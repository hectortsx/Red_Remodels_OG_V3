## Executive summary

The site is a public-facing U.S. home-remodeling business website. The production pages are static HTML and the contact forms send personal information to the business by email. Google Analytics 4 is used, maintained pages now serve fonts locally, and Cloudflare Turnstile now protects the maintained contact forms. Google reCAPTCHA was removed during the October 28 migration; older dated evidence below describes the prior state.

This review found no Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, cookie notice, or Accessibility Statement in the current public pages. Forms collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, and project messages without a nearby privacy notice. The site does not document retention, deletion, or privacy-request procedures. GA4 now loads only after a visitor accepts analytics through the deployed preference controls; rejection and later withdrawal are supported. Turnstile preloads when a visitor focuses a maintained form and runs verification on submission. Whether opt-in consent is legally required depends on visitor locations, applicable laws, and the final cookie/data inventory; do not assume the answer without checking.

At initial review, sampled live responses lacked common security headers. A CloudFront response headers policy is now deployed on the default site and `/api/*` behaviors; `Content-Security-Policy` remains unset pending compatibility review.

The pages already use several helpful accessibility patterns, including document language, semantic landmarks, visible labels around form controls, live status messaging, and a new keyboard-visible skip link on current pages. This source review did not test full keyboard/screen-reader use, contrast, touch target sizes, zoom/reflow, or conformance with a WCAG version. No compliance claim should be made until those checks are complete.

## Existing positive controls observed

- Production pages use HTTPS, have current canonical URLs, and the site publishes `robots.txt` and a sitemap.
- Contact inputs have associated visible labels, native required/email validation, and `autocomplete` values for common personal information.
- Contact submission status uses `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"`.
- The deployed Lambda validates required fields and email syntax, sanitizes email output, and requires Turnstile verification plus the CloudFront origin guard before sending email.
- Infrastructure documentation describes a private S3 origin behind CloudFront and records an origin encryption setting.

These controls are useful but do not establish legal compliance or complete security/accessibility conformance.
